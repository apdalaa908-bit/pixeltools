Questions

Will converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. A JPG has already lost detail, and PNG keeps what is there without losing more. Expect a larger file, not a sharper picture.

Why convert at all?

PNG can be edited and saved again without further quality loss, and many editors and sites require it.
