How to use Rotate and Flip

  1. Choose or drop an image.
  2. Adjust the settings if you need to.
  3. Check the preview, then download the result.

Questions

Does rotating reduce quality?

The image is redrawn once. PNG stays lossless; JPG and WebP are re-encoded at high quality.

Can I mirror an image?

Yes. Use the flip buttons to mirror it horizontally or vertically.
